Welcome to CampusBake®

We are a student-run bakery located at the Faculty of Computer Science and Mathematics cafeteria. Everything is baked fresh every morning — our cupcakes are RM 3.00 each and our cake slices are RM 5.00 each. Pre-orders must be placed at least 24 hours before pick-up, so please read the pick-up rules before you order.

Why pre-order with us?

  • No queue — your box is ready with your name on it.
  • Free delivery to any faculty office for orders above 20 pieces.
  • Halal ingredients, baked in a certified campus kitchen.

Pick-Up Information

Collect your order at Counter B, Cafeteria Level 1 between 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday. Orders that are not collected within 2 hours of the chosen slot will be released for walk-in sale.
